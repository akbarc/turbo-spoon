### Overview
This suite of analytics scripts provides deep business intelligence and financial metrics to support Georgia Wholesale's $4.6M commercial loan application to United Bankshares for the Tucker Hub acquisition.

### Scripts Included

#### 1. `bank_master_analytics.py` - **START HERE**
The master orchestration script that runs all critical validations.

- Generates executive summary with loan recommendation
- Produces JSON report with all findings
- **Runtime:** 5-10 minutes

#### 2. `bank_advanced_analytics.py` - Strategic Insights
Advanced business intelligence revealing:
- Customer velocity and engagement metrics
- Cash conversion excellence indicators
- Market position validation
- Predictive growth indicators
- **Purpose:** Demonstrates sophisticated business understanding

#### 3. `bank_hidden_patterns.py` - Competitive Advantages
Discovers non-obvious patterns including:

- Environmental correlations (weather, events)
- Product DNA and gateway product analysis
- Network effects and viral growth mechanics
- **Purpose:** Shows deep market knowledge and data sophistication

#### 4. `bank_financial_ratios.py` - Financial Strength
Calculates banking-specific metrics:
- Working capital efficiency ratios
- Return on invested capital (ROIC)
- Debt service coverage ratios (DSCR)
- Operating leverage indicators
- Banking covenant projections
- **Purpose:** Proves financial capacity to service debt

#### 5. `bank_competitive_moat.py` - Market Dominance
Analyzes sustainable competitive advantages:
- Customer lock-in and switching costs
- Market share and penetration metrics
- Economic moat depth and barriers to entry
- Growth runway validation
- **Purpose:** Demonstrates business durability and protection

#### 6. `bank_demo_queries.py` - Basic Validation
Original validation script for core metrics:
- Annual revenue verification
- Customer base analysis
- Product mix breakdown
- AR aging and collection rates
- **Purpose:** Quick validation of basic claims
